# What this course does not teach

Every item below is real, and every one is out.

| Out | Why, and when to pick it up |
|---|---|
| **Classic SEO fundamentals** (keyword research, Core Web Vitals, link building) | Strategists arrive with them; Builders do not need them to build or measure. Learn them after the course, in that order |
| **Embeddings and dense retrieval inside the engine** | Taught properly in *Retrieval Under Test*. The panel is lexical, and the content lessons here hold for dense engines too; week 7 checks on live ones |
| **Influencing what models learn in training** | It takes months to show and cannot be measured in eight weeks. Week 3 teaches you to choose whether training crawlers may read you, which is the part you control |
| **Ads inside answer engines** | Paid placement is a different job with different tools |
| **Agentic shopping and checkout protocols** | Moving every month. Read the vendors' documentation after week 8, with the audit habits from week 3 |
| **Images, video, voice, local and maps answers** | One medium done properly first. The method transfers |
| **Multilingual AEO** | The same method per language, after the course |
| **Hidden text, cloaking and prompt injection to win citations** | Never as tactics. Week 6 teaches you to detect them, on your site and on a competitor's |
| **Vendor AEO dashboards** | Week 7 builds the thing they sell. Buy one only once you know what it measures |

---

## Why

Each of these is learnable in about a week once you can measure a site's visibility in
an answer engine, attribute a loss to one step of the engine, and show a change helped.
None of them teaches you to. People who know why something was cut stop worrying that
they are missing it.

---

## The one that will be argued about

**Training data.** Being in a model's training data is the most-discussed "AEO" topic
and gets no lab here. The argument for cutting it: you cannot run the experiment. A
model trained next year on your page today gives you no result inside a course, and
anyone who claims a measured effect is measuring something else. The concession: it is
real, and week 3 makes you decide, in writing, whether to let training crawlers in.

---

## What to add afterwards, in order

1. **Classic technical SEO**, if you are a Builder. Engines that cite still mostly
   retrieve from a search index.
2. **Dense retrieval**, if you are a Strategist. *Retrieval Under Test*, weeks 5 to 7.
3. **Your industry's structured data** (product feeds, local business, medical or
   financial markup) with the week 5 method: test before you trust it.
