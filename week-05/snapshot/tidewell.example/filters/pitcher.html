The Tidewell Pitcher

Updated 2026-03-14

It is the filter most of our customers start with, and the one we are proudest of. Fill it from the tap, wait a few minutes, pour.

Why people love it

This is the part customers tell us about first: the water simply tastes cleaner. The carbon cartridge takes out the chlorine taste and smell, and it reduces lead too, which matters in older homes. The pitcher holds 2.4 litres.

Cartridges

They are easy to swap and click into place. Each one is good for about two months of normal use, which is around 150 litres. Replacement cartridges cost $12, or $10 each on a subscription.

Certification

It has been tested by an independent lab against NSF/ANSI Standard 42 for chlorine taste and odour and Standard 53 for lead. The pitcher does not remove PFAS, fluoride or dissolved minerals.

Price

The Tidewell Pitcher costs $34 and comes with one cartridge.
