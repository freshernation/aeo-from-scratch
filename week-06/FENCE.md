# Week 6 — Concept fence

## Allowed

**Everything from weeks 1 to 5**, plus:

- Mentions, inbound links, corroboration across domains
- Fact claims by subject, kind and nearby word; candidate contradictions
- Page dates; staleness against the panel's today (2026-10-01)
- Hidden text (display:none, visibility:hidden, hidden, aria-hidden); phrases aimed at AI
- Cloaking: what a crawler and a browser receive, compared
- The panel's trace (`trace["all"]`) and attribution to one step

## Not yet

Live engines and server logs (week 7) · asking other sites to change anything for real
(week 8, on your own site) · using any of the manipulation tactics (never)

---

## Why detect manipulation at all

A student who can spot hidden text and cloaking can explain a competitor's odd citation
to a client, and can stop an agency shipping either on the client's site. Both tactics
work on the panel because it does not check; that is a fact about the panel, not a
recommendation.
