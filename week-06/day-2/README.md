## What you need to know

### Candidates, not conflicts

A pattern finds prices and volumes; it cannot tell a product's price from its cartridge's,
or "costs $150" from "under $150". The finder lists every value it sees for a subject;
you decide which disagree about the same thing.

### Today's finding

**For the UX3's price the finder lists four values: $149, $150, $189 and $59. One is a
contradiction: the May 2024 review's $149 against Tidewell's $189. For the pitcher's
cartridge it lists three: 100, 150 and 2.4 litres. One is real: a 2023 forum post's
100 litres. 2.4 litres is the jug.** Two real conflicts out of seven values: report the
precision with the findings.

### Asking well

Ask an editor for an update with a source, reply on a forum as a named account, and use a
directory's correction form. Never ask anyone to delete a review or a member's post.
