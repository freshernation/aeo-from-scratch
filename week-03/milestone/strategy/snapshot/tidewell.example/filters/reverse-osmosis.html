RO-5 reverse osmosis system

Updated 2026-04-02

The RO-5 is our most thorough filter.

How it works

Water is pushed through a membrane with pores small enough to stop dissolved salts and metals. It is certified to NSF/ANSI Standard 58.

What it removes

Reverse osmosis removes fluoride, nitrates, arsenic, PFAS and most dissolved solids, as well as everything a carbon filter removes. It also removes calcium and magnesium, so the water tastes flatter.

Water use

This one is worth knowing before you buy. The RO-5 sends about one litre down the drain for every litre it filters.

Price

The RO-5 costs $399. Membranes last about two years.
