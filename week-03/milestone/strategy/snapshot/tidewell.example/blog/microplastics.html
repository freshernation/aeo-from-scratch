Microplastics in drinking water

Updated 2021-04-19

Microplastics are pieces of plastic smaller than 5 millimetres.

Do filters catch them

They are big enough that most carbon block filters with a pore size of 0.5 microns or less will catch the majority of them. Pitcher filters with loose carbon granules are less reliable.
