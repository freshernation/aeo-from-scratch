PFAS in tap water: what filters remove them

Updated 2026-02-18

PFAS are a family of thousands of man-made chemicals that do not break down in the environment, which is why they are called forever chemicals.

Which filters remove PFAS

Reverse osmosis and good carbon block filters both reduce PFAS. Reverse osmosis removes the widest range. A carbon block filter certified to NSF/ANSI Standard 53 for PFOA and PFOS reduces those two. Most pitcher filters are not certified for PFAS at all, including ours.

How to check a filter

Look up the model on the NSF listings and check that PFOA and PFOS appear under the standard. A claim on the box without a certificate number is a claim, not a test.
