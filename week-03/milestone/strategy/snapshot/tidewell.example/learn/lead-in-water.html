Lead in water

Updated 2026-01-12

Lead gets into tap water from old pipes and solder, not from the water source.

Which filters remove lead

A filter certified to NSF/ANSI Standard 53 for lead reduces it. Our pitcher and the UX3 are both certified. Boiling water does not remove lead.

Who should worry

Homes built before 1986 are the most likely to have lead pipes or solder.
