# Week 8 — Your own site (Project 3)

> **Destination**
> On a site you may change, you can choose thirty questions its customers ask, snapshot
> it and its competitors, audit it, make the three changes the audit ranks highest without
> inventing a fact, show the result on two engines, and book the live check, then defend
> all of it in a mock interview.

Seven weeks on Tidewell, where every answer could be checked. This week nobody has
written up the answers. The tools are the ones you built or used; the method is the one
from week 1: attribute the loss to one step before you change anything.

Both tracks do the same project. Builders use their own audit tool and monitor; Strategists
use the given scripts. There are no `build/` and `strategy/` folders this week.
