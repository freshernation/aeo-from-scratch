## What you need to know

### One report, re-run forever

The baseline is not a document; it is a command you run again after every change.
Builders generate it; Strategists write the version a person reads, and in week 3 their
numbers come from the Builders' kind of report.

### By intent

Tidewell is cited in 92% of answers to brand questions, 79% of informational ones, 50%
of comparisons, 40% of how-to questions and 35% of commercial ones: weakest exactly
where people are choosing what to buy.

### What a report must not hide

The interval, the denominator, what the panel is (not ChatGPT), what the prompts are
(our guess at customer questions), and that a citation is not a click.
