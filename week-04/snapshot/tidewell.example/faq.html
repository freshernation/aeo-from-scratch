Frequently asked questions

Updated 2026-05-01

How often should I change my pitcher cartridge?

Great question, and one we hear a lot. Our customers lead busy lives, so we designed the cartridge to be simple to change. Every 150 litres, or about every two months, is right for most homes.

Does the Tidewell Pitcher remove fluoride?

We get this one often. Fluoride is a small ion that carbon does not hold onto. Only the RO-5 removes fluoride.

Do you ship outside the US?

We ship to the US and Canada.
