The Tidewell shower filter

Updated 2025-11-10

It fits between the pipe and the shower head in about five minutes, with no tools.

What it does

It reduces chlorine using KDF media and calcium sulfite, which many people find kinder to skin and hair. It does not soften hard water.

Lifespan and price

A cartridge lasts six months. The filter costs $45 and a cartridge costs $18.
