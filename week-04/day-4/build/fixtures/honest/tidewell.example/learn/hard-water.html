Hard water

Updated 2024-02-08

Water filters do not soften hard water, except reverse osmosis: carbon filters leave the calcium and magnesium in place, and a water softener uses ion exchange instead.

Do filters soften hard water?

Carbon water filters do not soften hard water; reverse osmosis removes most of the calcium and magnesium that make water hard.
