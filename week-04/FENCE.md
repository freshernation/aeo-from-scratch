# Week 4 — Concept fence

## Allowed

**Everything from weeks 1 to 3**, plus:

- `aeolab.web2()` and snapshots laid over it; `aeolab.engine_b.EngineB`
- Answer-first, self-contained passages; the 12-word floor; question headings
- Facts by pattern: prices, percentages, volumes, standards, durations, years
- Your engine with the panel's boosts (B); a crawler built from week 3's pieces (B)
- Measuring a rewrite on several engines, paired by prompt, on first-cited

## Not yet

Structured data and JSON-LD (week 5) · other sites' claims about Tidewell (week 6) ·
live engines (week 7) · inventing statistics, quotes or testimonials (never)

---

## Why measure first-cited this week

After week 3 Tidewell is cited somewhere in 84% of the panel's answers. A rewrite has
little room to move that number and a lot of room to move which source comes first. A
metric that cannot move tells you nothing about a change.
