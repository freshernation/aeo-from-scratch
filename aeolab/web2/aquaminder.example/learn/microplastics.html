Do water filters remove microplastics?

Updated 2026-06-20

Carbon block filters rated at 0.5 microns and reverse osmosis systems remove most microplastics; loose-granule pitcher filters remove fewer.

What the research says

A 2024 review found microplastics in 81% of tap water samples tested worldwide.
