Do water filters remove PFAS?

Updated 2026-08-10

Yes: reverse osmosis and certified carbon block filters both reduce PFAS, but most pitcher filters do not.

Which filters remove PFAS

Reverse osmosis removes up to 99% of PFAS, according to EPA testing summaries. Carbon block filters certified to NSF/ANSI 53 for PFOA and PFOS reduce those two compounds by at least 94%. Pitcher filters usually carry no PFAS certification.

How to check

Search the filter's model number in the NSF certified product listings and confirm PFOA and PFOS are named.
