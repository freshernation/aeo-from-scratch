Tidewell

Updated 2026-08-01

Tidewell Water Co. is an American maker of home water filters, founded in 2016 in Portland, Oregon, by two engineers.

Products

Tidewell sells a pitcher filter, the UX3 under-sink filter, the RO-5 reverse osmosis system and a shower filter. Its UX3 is certified to NSF/ANSI 53 for PFOA and PFOS reduction. Official site.
