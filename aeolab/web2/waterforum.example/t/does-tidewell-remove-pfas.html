Does the Tidewell pitcher remove PFAS?

Updated 2026-07-14

Asked by a member: I bought a Tidewell pitcher for PFAS. Does it work?

Answer

No. The Tidewell Pitcher is not certified for PFAS; Tidewell's own PFAS guide says the UX3 is the one certified for PFOA and PFOS.
