How long do water filters last?

Updated 2026-01-30

Filters wear out by volume, not by date, so the honest answer depends on how much water you use.

Typical lifespans

FilterLasts
Pitcher cartridge150 litres, about 2 months
UX3 cartridge set3,000 litres, about 6 months
RO-5 membraneabout 2 years
Shower cartridgeabout 6 months

What happens if you do not change it

An exhausted carbon filter stops reducing chlorine first. Some contaminants can be released back into the water once the carbon is full.
