Carbon or reverse osmosis?

Updated 2025-12-01

It depends on what is in your water.

The short version

This is the question we get asked most. If your worry is taste, chlorine or lead, carbon is enough and wastes no water. If you need fluoride, nitrates or the widest PFAS reduction removed, you need reverse osmosis.

Cost

Carbon filters cost less to buy and run. Reverse osmosis costs more, wastes water and needs a drain connection.
