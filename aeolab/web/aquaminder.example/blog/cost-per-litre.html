What does filtered water cost per litre?

Updated 2026-04-11

Filtered water costs 2 to 8 cents per litre with a pitcher and 1 to 3 cents per litre with an under-sink filter, counting cartridges only.

Example

A $12 cartridge rated for 150 litres costs 8 cents per litre. A $59 cartridge set rated for 3,000 litres costs 2 cents per litre.
