New PFAS limits for tap water: what changes

Updated 2026-04-22

Federal limits for PFOA and PFOS in public drinking water are 4 parts per trillion each, and utilities have until 2029 to comply.

What households can do

Until then, a filter certified for PFOA and PFOS, such as ClearFlow's under-sink filter or Tidewell's UX3, reduces exposure.
