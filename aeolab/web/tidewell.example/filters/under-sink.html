UX3 under-sink filter

Updated 2026-05-20

This is the filter for people who want filtered water from a dedicated tap without thinking about it.

Three stages

It uses three stages: a sediment pre-filter, a carbon block, and a polishing stage. Together they handle the things people worry about most, and the flow is fast enough to fill a pan.

What it removes

It is certified to NSF/ANSI Standards 42, 53 and 401, and to Standard 53 for PFOA and PFOS reduction. That covers chlorine, lead, PFOA and PFOS, and several pharmaceuticals. It does not remove fluoride.

Cartridges and price

Each cartridge set lasts six months or 3,000 litres. The UX3 costs $189 and a replacement set costs $59.
