Do water filters remove lead?

Updated 2026-08-10

Yes: any filter certified to NSF/ANSI 53 for lead reduces it by at least 99% at the test concentration.

Which filters

Most certified pitchers, under-sink carbon filters and reverse osmosis systems reduce lead. Boiling does not remove lead; it concentrates it.
