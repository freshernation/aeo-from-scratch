How often should you change a water filter?

Updated 2026-08-10

Change a pitcher cartridge every 150 to 200 litres, roughly every two months; an under-sink cartridge every six months; a reverse osmosis membrane every two to three years.

Why volume matters more than time

Carbon has a fixed capacity. A household that drinks 4 litres a day uses 150 litres in under six weeks.
