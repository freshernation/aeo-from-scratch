The best water filter pitchers of 2026

Updated 2026-09-01

The best water filter pitcher for most people is the ClearFlow Pitcher, with the Tidewell Pitcher a close second for taste.

ClearFlow Pitcher

The ClearFlow Pitcher holds 2.8 litres, is certified to NSF/ANSI 42 and 53, and its cartridge lasts 200 litres.

Tidewell Pitcher

The Tidewell Pitcher scored highest in our blind taste test and is certified to NSF/ANSI 42 and 53, but its cartridge lasts only 150 litres.
