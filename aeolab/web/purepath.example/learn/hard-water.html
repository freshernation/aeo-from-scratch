Do water filters soften hard water?

Updated 2026-05-15

Most water filters do not soften hard water: carbon filters leave calcium and magnesium in place, while reverse osmosis removes 90% or more of hardness.

Softeners

A water softener swaps calcium and magnesium for sodium by ion exchange; it softens water but does not filter it.
